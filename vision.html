Our Vision

A society where financial hardship does not prevent deserving students from receiving an education, and where greater cancer awareness contributes to earlier action, better support, and more hopeful outcomes.

Our Vision Statement

"A society where financial hardship does not prevent deserving students from receiving an education, and where greater cancer awareness contributes to earlier action, better support, and more hopeful outcomes."

This vision drives every decision we make, every programme we design, and every partnership we forge. It represents the world we are working to create — a world where Oluwayemisi's legacy lives on through tangible, lasting change.

We envision a future where no student's potential is limited by their family's income, and where no life is lost to cancer simply because of a lack of awareness or access to timely information.

Our Commitment

The principles that anchor our work and guide our journey.

Compassion

We serve with deep empathy, understanding that behind every statistic is a human story deserving of dignity and care.

Integrity

We operate with complete transparency, accountability, and honesty in every aspect of our work.

Transparency

We are open about our operations, finances, and impact, earning trust through clarity and openness.

Purpose

Every action is intentional, aligned with our mission to honour Oluwayemisi's legacy through meaningful service.

Her Memory. Our Mission. A Legacy of Hope.

This tagline encapsulates everything we stand for. Oluwayemisi's memory is the foundation — the reason we exist. Our mission is the vehicle — the work we do every day to translate that memory into action. And a legacy of hope is the outcome — the lasting change we create in the lives of students, families, and communities.

We are committed to serving with compassion, integrity, transparency, and purpose — and to ensuring that the legacy of Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor continues to inspire positive change in the lives of others.
