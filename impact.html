Impact

A Woman of Impact. A Life That Touched Other Lives.

Her Story

Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor lived a life that was defined not only by what she achieved, but by the lives she touched along the way.

She was a woman of warmth, compassion, purpose, and impact — a woman whose presence made a difference to her family, friends, colleagues, and the many people whose paths crossed hers.

Though her life was cut short by breast cancer, her impact did not end with her passing. It lives on in the memories she created, the people she inspired, and the lives that will be touched through the work of the Foundation established in her honour.

This is the story of a life lived with purpose.
A life that mattered.
A true woman of impact.

Journey of Impact

Key milestones in our journey of turning compassion into action.

Foundation Established

The Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor Foundation was officially established in loving memory of Oluwayemisi, transforming personal loss into a legacy of service.

First Scholarships Awarded

Our inaugural scholarship recipients were selected — deserving students who demonstrated academic promise despite financial hardship.

First School Awareness Programme

Launched our breast cancer awareness programme in secondary schools, educating young people on early detection and health advocacy.

Community Outreach Expansion

Expanded our public awareness campaigns to multiple communities, partnering with healthcare providers for free screenings and education.

Partnership Network Growth

Formed strategic partnerships with corporations, healthcare institutions, and community organisations to amplify our reach and impact.

Ongoing: Building the Legacy

Continuing to grow our programmes, reach more students, educate more communities, and honour Oluwayemisi's memory through service.
