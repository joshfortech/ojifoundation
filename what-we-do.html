What We Do

Turning Compassion Into Action

Our Programmes

At the Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor Foundation, we believe that meaningful change begins when people are given opportunity, knowledge, and hope.

Our programmes are centred on two key areas: educational scholarships and cancer awareness and advocacy. Through these initiatives, we seek to make a practical difference in the lives of individuals and communities while keeping Oluwayemisi's memory alive through service.

Educational Scholarships

Giving Students the Opportunity to Dream

Financial hardship should not be a barrier to education or a reason for a young person to abandon their aspirations. The Foundation provides educational scholarships to indigent and deserving students who demonstrate the commitment and potential to pursue their education but face significant financial challenges.

Our scholarship support is designed to help ease the financial burden associated with education and give beneficiaries the opportunity to remain in school, develop their abilities, and work towards a brighter future. We see every scholarship not simply as financial assistance, but as an investment in a life and in the future of a family and community.

What Our Scholarships Provide

  • ✓ Tuition and examination fees
  • ✓ Educational materials and resources
  • ✓ Mentorship and guidance support
  • ✓ Ongoing academic monitoring

Cancer Awareness & Advocacy

Knowledge Can Save Lives

Cancer can affect anyone, but a lack of awareness can make its impact even more devastating. Drawing inspiration from Oluwayemisi's own experience with breast cancer, the Foundation is committed to promoting cancer awareness, education, and advocacy, with particular attention to breast cancer.

Our Awareness Initiatives Seek To:

  • ● Promote knowledge about cancer and its risk factors
  • ● Encourage people to pay attention to changes in their bodies and seek appropriate medical advice
  • ● Promote awareness of the importance of early detection and timely medical attention
  • ● Help dispel myths, misconceptions, fear, and stigma surrounding cancer
  • ● Encourage conversations about cancer within families and communities
  • ● Advocate for greater understanding, support, and access to appropriate cancer-related information and care

Our Approach

We believe lasting impact is built through partnership and community. We therefore seek to work with schools, healthcare professionals, community organisations, individuals, corporations, and other stakeholders who share our commitment to improving lives.

Key Programmes

Public Awareness Campaigns

Community outreaches and public events raising awareness about breast cancer and early detection.

School Awareness Programmes

Dedicated sessions in schools educating young people about breast health and cancer prevention.

Healthcare Partnerships

Collaborations with medical professionals for screenings, referrals, and expert guidance.

Community Advocacy

Empowering communities to advocate for better cancer care access and support systems.
