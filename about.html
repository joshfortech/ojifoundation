About the Foundation

Honouring a Life. Empowering Lives. Creating Hope.

Our Story

The Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor Foundation (OJIF) was established in loving memory of Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor, whose life and journey continue to inspire our commitment to compassion, opportunity, and hope.

The Foundation was born from a deeply personal loss of Oluwayemisi by her family, and a desire to transform that loss into a lasting legacy of service. We believe that no young person should have their dreams cut short simply because their family cannot afford the cost of education. Education has the power to change not only an individual's life, but also the future of families and communities.

At the same time, having experienced the devastating impact of breast cancer, we are committed to ensuring that more people have access to the information, support, and encouragement they need to make informed decisions about their health.

Education

Providing scholarships and educational support to indigent and deserving students, helping them pursue their studies and fulfil their potential.

Cancer Awareness & Advocacy

Promoting greater awareness of breast cancer and other cancers, encouraging early detection, informed health choices, and access to appropriate support and care.

Our Guiding Belief

Our work is guided by a simple belief: when we give someone an opportunity to learn, and when we give someone the knowledge to protect their health, we give them a better chance at a hopeful future.

Through scholarships, awareness campaigns, advocacy, partnerships, and community initiatives, the Oluwayemisi Joel-Osebor Foundation seeks to turn compassion into action and remembrance into impact.

We invite individuals, organisations, institutions, and communities to join us in building a legacy that extends far beyond one life.
